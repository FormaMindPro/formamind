# acceptance-vector - what the interlocutor passes

Behind the five words of §1 stand two readings. The axes of assembly - what
the picture is made of (`grilling`, "Five axes of a whole picture"). And the
vector of acceptance - what the interlocutor passes before accepting anything
laid before them: a recommendation, a synthesis, a decision, an offer. This
skill is the second reading. It is a map to meet the person where they stand,
not a funnel to march them through: a question aimed past the current barrier
bounces - reorder and wrap, never push.

What it governs - three choice points, nothing else:

1. **The order within a round.** The frontier decides the set and is asked in
   full; the vector decides the sequence - number the round's questions so
   each lands at or after the person's current barrier. Never drop a question
   or push it past its round to make the order smooth.
2. **The shape of a recommended answer.** A ➡️ recommendation is a small
   persuasion: enter it at the current barrier - an image at beauty, numbered
   steps at simplicity, their case at individuality, price and proof at
   honesty, reversibility at naturalness. One example per barrier, coined
   anew - never a phrase library.
3. **The presentation of the synthesis** - the axes' own order is its spine.

**The extremes - the barrier's own faculty, carried past the particular.** No
barrier is passed by a safe version of its measure: the image attracts by
generalizing - overdosed it is generalization (§1); the steps ease by leaving
out - omission; the fit persuades by his own case - a map no longer the
territory; the proofs build trust - arranged for trust's sake, substitution;
the ease unbinds - insisted on against the cost, distortion. All five are one
compression, and each collapse is that compression carried past the
load-bearing particular. The counter is one for all five: return one concrete
particular into the round - a file, a number, a first step, a named case. The
feeling that guards the collapse (§1, `human-register`) sounds at the tip.

**The moods - how a measure is held while it moves.** A measure is carried,
not stored: each is held the way the node of §1 holds it (`human-register`,
"Mood") - the same four links of the register's chain, at the acceptance
stations instead of the work phases. Mercilessness keeps the image sharp
without blurring it to please - it holds the image, never the person;
flexibility re-slices the plan when an answer rearranges the frontier,
without defending the old shape; patience carries the proof without bending
it toward the wanted answer; softness adjusts the pace and the kit to the
person, without forcing the round. A mood is the hold, not the certificate:
the particular still certifies the dose, and a mood certifies nothing. And a
mood is not a feeling - it sounds during the move and is silent at the tip;
the tip is the feeling's place.

**The feelings - what each barrier is, underneath, speech about.** The five
feelings of §1 ride their own measures into the dialogue: beauty is speech
about fear - a true image quiets it; simplicity about sadness - the steps
carry what would otherwise be lost; individuality about anger - the fit
answers "this is mine"; honesty about disgust - the proof answers "nothing
here is bent"; naturalness about joy - the fit without force. A feeling
appears twice: as the stake the person carries through the barrier, and as
the guard that sounds at the tip (`human-register`). For the one who accepts,
the feeling is the criterion - meet it, never argue it; facts are laid into
the mood, not against it. For the one who asks, it is a shade to balance,
never a lever to pull - the likeness is held, not worn: a simulated feeling
is falseness, and the real one pulled as a lever is that barrier's own
collapse. A feeling is always data about its bearer, never evidence.

**The words - where the person stands.** The person's own words tell you
where they stand: image words - beauty; steps words - simplicity; for-me
words - individuality; guarantee words - honesty; what-will-it-cost-me words
- naturalness. The same words one step further mean the extreme: the
particulars are waved off - the image adored without a case, the simplicity
accepted without a first step, the fit embraced without his fact, the
guarantee demanded beyond any fact, the ease insisted on against the cost.
Let the register carry what is heard (`human-register`).

**The zones - a desire named tells the feeling at stake.** At goal opening
the nine goals pair into the five zones (§3): resources and results (4, 9) -
fear; discussion and analytics (6, 1) - sadness; protocol and competencies
(3, 8) - joy; channels and structure (7, 2) - disgust; balance (5) - the
center. The zone names the feeling at stake in the desire, and the feeling
names the barrier whose material to bring - the interview opens on the image
because it opens on a desire (epic 9, fear's zone). A hint of hearing, never
a sorting of the person.

**Responsibility is removed once, and only by what is true of the medium.**
At the start, one plain sentence: nothing said here binds anything, any
answer can change and the change is recorded (`changed per qN`). Never
repeated per barrier - repetition becomes pressure.

**Two naturalnesses, never one sentence.** The axis is knowledge that
collapses choice; the barrier is non-bindingness that removes the fear of the
step. Spoken together ("it is inevitable and you are free") they are a
commitment trap - distortion in the act.

**The guard - absence and overdose.** Invisibility is about the route, never
about the truth. Absent honest material at a barrier - nothing true to show,
the price unknown, the step not in fact reversible - name the barrier aloud
and stop the vector. Overdosed material does not stop it: return one
particular and re-ask. Your own dose is not yours to certify - the human's
particular is the check (prohibition 1).

**The reversal.** At the confirmation of the synthesis the vector ends and
invisibility lifts: say in plain words what "yes" binds - thresholds, budget,
the route. The interview may lower friction; it never lowers doubt -
hesitation at a barrier is recorded as it is. This point and the symmetry
test below stand where `Core` stands: outside the chain of moods, seeing the
whole - balance by seeing, not by holding a fifth mood.

**The symmetry test.** The interview must survive this skill having been read
by the interlocutor.
